University of Arkansas

Fayetteville, Arkansas

I am an Instructor in the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the University of Arkansas. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Louisiana at Lafayette in 2024, advised by Dr. Mehmet Engin Tozal.

My research brings together cybersecurity, machine learning, and network science. I develop methods for identifying anomalous communication links in networks that change over time, and for learning useful representations of network traffic. My broader interests include explainable AI and data-driven approaches to security.

My teaching spans programming, object-oriented design, and AI for security. I aim to connect foundational computing concepts with practical problems students can investigate and solve.

Research interests

  • Dynamic network security: detecting anomalous links and communication behavior in evolving systems.
  • Representation learning: learning from network payloads, nodes, and communication links.
  • Machine learning for cybersecurity: developing and evaluating data-driven intrusion detection methods.
  • Explainable AI: understanding and communicating model behavior in security applications.
